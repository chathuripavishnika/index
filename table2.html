FOOD GALARY

Food Categories

Pizza is a popular Italian food made with dough, tomato sauce, cheese and different toppings. It is enjoyed by people all around the world.

Burger is a popular fast food made with a bread bun, meat or vegetables, cheese and different sauces. It is a favourite meal for many people.

Pasta is a famous Italian food made from wheat flour and water. It can be prepared with different sauces, vegetables and meat.

Cake is a sweet food usually made with flour, sugar, eggs and butter. Cakes are commonly served at birthdays and special occasions.
